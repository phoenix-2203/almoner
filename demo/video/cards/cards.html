The question

What happens when an AI agent with money is hacked, tricked, or simply wrong?

The problem

A spending limit caps the damage.
It doesn't stop these.

01The wrong purchase

Inside the budget, but nothing to do with the task. A poisoned web page is enough.

02A changed price or payee

The agent, or whoever controls it, edits what was agreed on the way to the wallet.

03A seller who never delivers

Payment is final the moment it is sent. Junk or nothing comes back.

How Almoner works · 2

The agent asks. The contract decides.

01 · QUOTESeller signs

Price, item and payee are signed. The vault verifies it with ed25519.

→
02 · DECIDEVault checks

Purpose, blocked terms, limits, budget. Approve, hold for the owner, or refuse.

→
03 · ESCROWMoney waits

Reserved in the vault until the seller delivers.

→
04 · SETTLEPay or refund

Accepted: seller paid. Rejected or late: refunded, seller put on hold.

Every decision, including every refusal, is written to a hash-chained log on-chain.
